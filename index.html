ABOUT US

Empowering Communities. Inspiring Change.

Zoe Tirzah Missions, in partnership with Tirzah Oasis Foundation and Zoe Worship Centre, is dedicated to bringing the love of Christ and holistic community relief to vulnerable, unreached towns across Ghana and beyond.

Gospel evangelism, church planting & youth discipleship
Free medical clinics, healthcare screenings & medicines
Community relief, clothing drives & nutritional support
Mobilizing local and international mission volunteers
