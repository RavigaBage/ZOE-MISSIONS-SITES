Our Story & Leadership

Turning Purpose into Impact.

Our vision is to see lives transformed, communities impacted, and generations empowered through the preaching of the Gospel of Jesus Christ and practical, compassionate service.

Executive Council Committee Heads

Gospel & Church Planting

Direct preaching of the Gospel, door-to-door evangelism, crusades, and planting vibrant local assemblies across rural communities in Ghana.

Free Medical Care

Deploying licensed medical doctors, nurses, and pharmacists to provide screenings, medication, eye checks, and health counseling.

Community Benevolence

Distributing thousands of clothing items, footwear, educational supplies, and nutritional provisions to rural families and vulnerable children.

Children & Youth Ministry

Vibrant Vacation Bible Schools, character molding, literacy support, and kingdom mentorship to nurture tomorrow's leaders.

Ecclesial Governance

The Apostolic Church - Ghana Partnership

Zoe Tirzah Missions operates under the auspices of Apostolic Zion Worship Centre (AZWC), a dynamic assembly within The Apostolic Church - Ghana. All missions are governed under the pastoral oversight and prayer cover of church leadership to ensure spiritual integrity, ethical stewardship, and lasting kingdom fruit.
