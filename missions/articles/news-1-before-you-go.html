Before You Go: How We Prepared for Missions 2026

Months of prayer, fasting and community visits came before a single soul was won. Here is what preparation taught us.

September 2, 20262 min readMissions 2026

Nobody sees the work that happens before a mission. They see the crusade lights, the crowds and the altar calls. But if you ask any of us who went to Nkroful and Teleku Bokazo, we will tell you the mission was already half won months before we boarded the buses.

Jesus once asked, "For which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and counteth the cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it?" (Luke 14:28). That verse was our working guide. We met to plan. We fasted and prayed. We split into fifteen sub-committees, from Evangelism and Medical to Food, Transport, Security and Sanitation, so that everyone knew what they owned. We visited the communities on 31st January and again on 18th July to meet local church leaders and secure grounds and accommodation.

Nehemiah did something similar. Before he rebuilt anything, he went out at night and looked at the walls himself (Nehemiah 2:12-15). A mission is no different. You cannot love a place you have not seen.

I will be honest about one thing. This year we made fewer visits to the mission grounds than in past years, and we felt it. We arrived less familiar with the field than we should have been. So that is lesson number one for anyone planning a mission: visit early, and visit more than once.

Do

  • Pray and fast as a team before you plan anything else.
  • Visit the community at least twice, and meet the local pastor or church leader first.
  • Give every person a clear job under a committee.
  • Count the cost honestly, including food, transport, medical supplies and a place for new believers to meet.
  • Apply for sponsorship early. A good letter sent late helps nobody.

Don’t

  • Don't arrive as strangers with a finished plan the community never heard about.
  • Don't treat the local church as a venue. They are your partners.
  • Don't leave training for the field. Teach evangelism, role-play it and correct it before you go.

"Without counsel purposes are disappointed: but in the multitude of counsellors they are established" (Proverbs 15:22). Plan with many hands, and let God do what only He can.
